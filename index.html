About WarpX

Your town's everything app, minus the wait.

WarpX is a hyperlocal pickup-and-drop service built specifically for small towns — not a scaled-down version of a big-city app, but one designed around how a compact town actually works: short distances, familiar landmarks, and things people need in minutes, not hours.

We run four dedicated services — food from Picasso Cafe, grocery, medicine, and laundry — each with its own page so ordering never feels cluttered. And because no catalog covers everything a town needs, every service also has a simple custom box: if it's not listed, just write it down and we'll get it delivered anyway.
