Why not just ask for an address?

A typed address is ambiguous for delivery routing in a small town where many streets share names or have none. Raw GPS coordinates are precise but meaningless to type or read. So WarpX gets the precise part from your phone's GPS automatically (one tap, no numbers shown), lets you nudge a pin if it's slightly off, and offers a landmark list for anyone who'd rather not share GPS at all — the address/landmark note is then just extra context for the delivery partner, not the source of truth for the zone check.
