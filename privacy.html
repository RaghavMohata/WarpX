Privacy policy

What we collect when you order or deliver with WarpX, why we need it, who sees it, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Who we are

WarpX is a local pickup and delivery service in Brahmapuri, Maharashtra, run from warpx.online. We deliver food, groceries, medicine, laundry and other errands, and we're responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us at raghavmohata24@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Name and mobile number, so riders can find you and call about an order.
  • Password. We keep only a scrambled version (a salted hash), never the password itself, so nobody at WarpX can read it.
  • Email address, if you add one or sign in with Google.

Where to deliver

  • Addresses you save, with the labels you give them (like "Home").
  • The map location of each address, taken from your phone's location if you allow it, or from a point you pick. We use it to work out delivery time and fee. We don't track your location in the background or when you aren't adding an address.

Your orders

  • What you order, including any notes you write, the price, delivery fee and total, and the delivery time you choose.
  • How you pay. Cash on delivery, or UPI. For UPI we record the UPI ID you type. The payment itself happens in your own UPI app, and we never see your bank or card details.
  • A 4-digit delivery code for each order, which you give the rider at handover to prove it reached you.
  • Prescriptions. If you choose a prescription file on the medicine page, only its file name goes with your order. The file itself stays on your phone.
  • Your weekly vegetable plan, if you use the weekly list.

If you deliver with us

  • What you tell us when you apply: name, mobile number, vehicle, area, availability and any notes.
  • Your deliveries and payouts, so we can pay you correctly.
  • Email address and Google account ID, if you link Google to the Driver Hub.

Signing in with Google

If you choose Continue with Google, Google tells us your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. We use them only to create your WarpX account or sign you in, and to fill in your name and email. We store them with your account and share them only as described under "Who can see your information".

We don't receive your Google password or anything else in your Google account. We don't keep any ongoing access to it either: Google confirms who you are once, each time you sign in.

How we use it

  • To take your order, deliver it, and contact you about it.
  • To show you your order history and saved addresses.
  • To plan delivery routes, and to pay riders for their deliveries.
  • To keep accounts secure. For example, we limit repeated wrong password attempts.

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who can see your information

  • The rider delivering your order sees your name, mobile number, delivery address and what you ordered. They never see your delivery code.
  • The restaurant or shop preparing your order sees only the items and your notes, not your name, number or address. For medicine that needs a prescription, our partner pharmacist may call you to confirm it.
  • The WarpX team sees orders and accounts in order to run the service.
  • Services that help us run WarpX:
    • Cloudflare carries all traffic between your phone and our server.
    • Google handles Google sign-in when you use it. The language button loads Google Translate, which sends the page's text to Google to translate it. The site's fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to load them.
    • Messaging and email services (such as WhatsApp, Telegram and email) carry order alerts to our team, riders and restaurants, with only the details each of them needs.
  • The authorities, if the law requires us to share information.

Cookies and storage on your phone

  • Sign-in cookies. When you log in we set one cookie that keeps you signed in, for up to 180 days for customers and 30 days for riders. It works only on warpx.online, and logging out removes it.
  • Your cart and preferences are saved in your own browser, so they're still there when you come back.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Google may set its own cookies when you use Google sign-in or the language button.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your account, saved addresses and order history while you use WarpX, so you can see past orders and we can sort out any problem with a delivery or a payment.

To delete your account, email raghavmohata24@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or tell us the mobile number it uses. Within 30 days, we'll delete your account and saved addresses and remove your name and number from past orders, except for anything the law requires us to keep. You can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or to correct it.

Keeping it safe

The site is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Passwords are stored scrambled, sign-in cookies can't be read by other websites or by scripts on the page, and repeated wrong password or delivery-code attempts are blocked for a while. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to collect only what a delivery needs.

Children

WarpX is meant for adults. If you're under 18, please use it only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions, a deletion request, or a complaint about your data: email raghavmohata24@gmail.com.
